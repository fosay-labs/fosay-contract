# Code of Conduct

FOSAY follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique code rather than people, and keep
discussion on topic. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not
tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/fosay-labs/fosay-contract/security/advisories/new)
(it reaches maintainers only) or by messaging a maintainer listed in the README. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and block participants who break
these rules.
